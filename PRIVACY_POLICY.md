# Privacy Policy for Immich Swipe

**Last updated: August 18, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how **Immich Swipe** ("the Application", "we", "us", or "our") handles your information when you use our mobile application. 

We take your privacy seriously. Immich Swipe is designed as a client-side tool for the Immich self-hosted photo engine.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use

### 1.1 Immich Server Data
The Application requires you to provide your Immich server URL and an API Key to function.
*   **Local Storage**: This information is stored **locally on your device** using secure Android preferences.
*   **No Third-Party Transmission**: We do not transmit any of your Immich credentials, photos, or metadata to our own servers or any third party. All communication is directly between the Application and your specified Immich server.

### 1.2 Photo and Media Access
The Application accesses your media stored on your Immich server to allow you to sort them. This data is processed on-device and on your server. We do not have access to your photos.

---

## 2. Third-Party Services (Play Store Version)

The version of Immich Swipe distributed via the Google Play Store includes third-party services to support development through advertising.

### 2.1 Google AdMob
We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use certain data, such as:
*   **Advertising ID (AAID)**: To provide personalized or non-personalized ads.
*   **Device Information**: To ensure ad delivery and prevent fraud.

For more information, please visit [Google’s Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

### 2.2 User Messaging Platform (UMP)
We use the Google UMP SDK to manage user consent in compliance with privacy regulations like GDPR. 
*   **Managing Preferences**: You can manage or revoke your consent at any time. To do so, go to the Application's **Settings**, scroll down to the **Privacy** section, and click on **Ad preferences**. This will reopen the consent form.

---

## 3. FOSS Version (GitHub / F-Droid)

The FOSS (Free and Open Source Software) version of the application is completely free of any advertising or tracking SDKs. No data is shared with Google or any advertising network in the FOSS flavor.

---

## 4. Data Security

We implement standard security measures to protect your locally stored API Key. However, the security of your data also depends on the security of your self-hosted Immich instance and the safety of your device.

---

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
